How Iceland's income tax brackets work

Tax applies progressively to taxable salary after deductible employee pension contributions. Crossing a threshold does not apply the higher rate to your entire salary.

Does this include pension and union fees?

Employee pension is included, defaulting to 4% of gross pay. Ísland.is explains the minimum employee contribution. Eligible pension contributions reduce taxable salary; see Skatturinn's deductions guidance.

Union fees (stéttarfélag), holiday-pay arrangements (orlof), benefits, other deductions and annual assessment adjustments are excluded. Municipal differences and individual circumstances can affect final tax. This tool estimates ordinary employment income, not self-employment, capital gains or special child-income rules.
